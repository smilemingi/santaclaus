How to play

  • Tap / Space drops a gift. Land it in the chimney of NICE kids.
  • Never deliver to NAUGHTY kids (-1 ❤). Skipping them gives coal points.
  • Gold mansions pay x2, Emerald x3, Diamond x5. Tap fast — rapid taps work!
  • Clear each stage by delivering enough gifts. Higher stages are faster and need more.
  • Hit floating balloons for power-ups, used instantly. A Shield is stored and used automatically when you'd lose a heart.
  • Hit the golden B-O-N-U-S letters for a 5-second gift rain with no penalties.
  • Every background has its own house style and music. Unlock sleighs by total score, backgrounds by deliveries and 🦌 Rudolphs (each with a power) by your best score. 🎁 Come back daily for a reward! 🌿 Try Mode 2 — Grinch Night: deliver COAL to the naughty kids!
